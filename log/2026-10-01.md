**What I did:** Determined Turbine inlet temp (which I will call T4) to be 606.7 Celcius (logged Oct 7)\
**Details:**
+ Since 713C Inconel has a temperature creep limit of around 920 Celcius, we will take the limit as 900 degrees celcius to be conservative
+ This means the T4 + margin for hot streaks (which are spikes above the average operating temp)  <= 900 Celcius
+ I took the margin to be 25 percent. 900 Celcius = 1173.15 Kelvin (which I am using since it is a more physically meaningful way to measure temp)
+ so T4 = 1173.15K *(1-0.25) = 879.86K or around 606.7 Celcius
