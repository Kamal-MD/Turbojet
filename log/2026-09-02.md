**What I did:** Changed compressor to chamber connector material from Aluminum to Silicone hose and modeled the silicone hose with a placeholder value\
**Details:**
+ Due to sourcing difficulties in Lebanon and the technical difficulty of making an Aluminum connector tube with a V-band flange, I decided to switch to using a silicone hose as a connector
+ This decision is due to the fact that silicone hoses can resist extreme temperatures of up to 250 degrees Celsius, which is a great margin over our projected compressor discharge temp of around 127 degrees (150 degrees in extreme cases and random spikes of temp)
+ The logic behind it is that I will buy a hose with an inner diameter slightly greater than that of the outer diameter of the compressor (which is 74mm) and use a hose clamp behind the lip to create an airtight seal
+ Ideally, I would by a hose of an inner diameter at the compressor end that differs from the diameter on the chamber end (Note: the hoses diameter at the compressor end is fixed at 75.5 mm, while the decision of having a smaller diameter at the chamber end can be subject to future change)
