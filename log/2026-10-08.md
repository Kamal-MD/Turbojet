## Combustor zone design: full-power point (with wall cooling)

**φ_overall = 0.189 (calculated), rounded to 0.19 for design. Wall cooling: 12% of total air.**

| Zone | φ (design) | Zone-hole air | Cooling air along wall | Cumulative air mixed into gas | T (formula) | Purpose |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Primary | 1.0 | 19.0% | 6% | 19.0% | ~2551 K | Stable flame in recirculation bubble |
| Secondary | 0.6 (provisional) | 12.7% | 4% | 31.7% | ~1775 K | Burn out CO left by primary zone |
| Dilution | 0.19 | 56.3% | 2% | 100% | ~882 K (609 °C) | Cool to T4 target, even out profile |
| **Total** | | **88%** | **12%** | | | |

**Design fuel flow:** ≈ 2.6 g/s propane

**Formulas:**
- f_zone = φ_zone × FAR_st
- T_zone = T3 + (f · LHV · η_b) / ((1 + f) · c_p)
- X_zone = φ_overall / φ_zone

**Assumptions:** propane, FAR_st = 0.0642, LHV = 46,350 kJ/kg, T3 = 400 K, ṁ_air = 0.212 kg/s, all fuel injected at the flange. c_p = 1.3 / 1.25 / 1.1 kJ/kg·K (primary / secondary / dilution). η_b = 1.0 for hot zones, 0.95 at exit. **Cooling air is assumed to stay as a wall film and not take part in zone φ; it mixes into the gas by the exit.**

**Notes:**
- Cooling placement (6 / 4 / 2%) is provisional, weighted toward the hottest walls.
- Dilution-hole air drops from 68.3% to 56.3%. Weaker dilution jets may not reach the center, risking a hot core and hot streaks at the turbine. Check in SimScale.
- In reality some cooling air gets pulled into the zone it borders. If half the primary-wall cooling (3%) mixed in, the primary zone would see 22% air and φ ≈ 0.86, leaner than designed, which reduces blowout margin at idle.
- Formula temperatures ignore dissociation; hot zones are overestimated.

The cooling holes beside the primary zone should be small and angled to lay air along the wall, specifically so it doesn't get drawn into the flame.
