**What I did:** Determined the φ in each zone, as well as the operating temp of each zone\
**Details:**
+ For the primary zone, we use φ = 1 since that is when the temperature, and therefore the combustion rate is highest. This means it can keep up with the fast flowing air without being extinguished
+ For secondary zone, we use φ = 0.6 (tentative), because it is what is used in most turbojet designs, and because its operating temperature is cool enough for CO2 to remain CO2 without dissociation but hot enough that CO burns into CO2
+ The rest of air goes to dilution, such that the overall  φ = 0.19
+ The operating temps and air splits in each zone are in the table below, which was generated by Claude, and Claude also aided in the calculations:

## Combustor zone design

**φ_overall = 0.189 (calculated), rounded to 0.19 for design.**

| Zone | φ (cumulative) | f | Cumulative air | Air added in zone | T (formula) | Expected real T | Purpose |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Primary | 1.0 | 0.0642 | 19.0% | 19.0% | ~2551 K | ~2350 K (Cantera to confirm) | Stable flame in recirculation bubble |
| Secondary | 0.6 (provisional) | 0.0385 | 31.7% | 12.7% | ~1775 K | Somewhat lower (Cantera to confirm) | Burn out CO left by primary zone |
| Dilution | 0.19 | 0.0122 | 100% | 68.3% | ~882 K (609 °C) | ~882 K | Cool to T4 target, even out profile |

**Design fuel flow:** 0.0122 × 0.212 kg/s ≈ 2.6 g/s propane

**Formulas:**
- f_zone = φ_zone × FAR_st
- T_zone = T3 + (f · LHV · η_b) / ((1 + f) · c_p)
- X_zone = φ_overall / φ_zone

**Assumptions:** propane, FAR_st = 0.0642, LHV = 46,350 kJ/kg, T3 = 400 K, ṁ_air = 0.212 kg/s, all fuel injected at the flange. c_p = 1.3 / 1.25 / 1.1 kJ/kg·K (primary / secondary / dilution). η_b = 1.0 for hot zones (ideal, comparable to Cantera), 0.95 at exit.

**Notes:**
- Rounding φ_overall up from 0.189 to 0.19 raises T4 from 879.9 K to ~882.4 K (+2.5 K), well inside the 25% margin to the 900 °C turbine limit.
- Primary and secondary temperatures depend only on each zone's own φ, so they're unaffected by the rounding.
- Formula temperatures ignore dissociation, so the hot zones are overestimated.
- Wall-cooling air is not yet allocated; scheduled for Oct 10. It will come out of the 68.3% dilution share.
