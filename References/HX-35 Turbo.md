**Holset HX-35 Turbocharger Specs**
+ **turbine inlet type:** "T3 divided, bolted, 4 bolts"
+ **turbine downpipe Outer Diameter:** 79 mm V-band OD, turbine downpipe side
+ **turbine downpipe Inner Diameter:** 65 mm V-band clamp bore, turbine downpipe side
+ **turbine wheel inlet:** 70 mm turbine wheel inducer (informational, not a mating dim)
+ **turbine wheel outlet:** 60 mm turbine wheel exducer (informational)
+ **turbine housing Area:** 12 cm², informational only, doesn't drive geometry
+ **compressor inlet bore:** 102 mm compressor housing intake
+ **compressor outlet Outer Diameter:** 74 mm V-band OD, compressor outlet
+ **compressor outlet Inner Diameter:** 52 mm V-band clamp bore (Inner Diameter), compressor outlet
+ **compressor wheel inlet:** 52 mm compressor wheel inducer (informational)
+ **compressor wheel outlet:** 77 mm compressor wheel exducer (informational)

**Note:**
+ Turbine Inducer is the where the gas/fluid enters the turbine wheel
+ Turbine Exducer is where it exits the turbine wheel (where turbine inducer diameter is generally larger than exducer diameter)
